### F7 — [stage-2] Concurrent logins bypass the 5-failure lockout — **Fix now**
- Where: `src/Rochambeau.Api/Modules/Identity/Application/LogIn/LogIn.cs:63,69,74-82,92-99,103`. AC: AC-06 (spec §6.1 password guessing). Raised by two reviewers.
